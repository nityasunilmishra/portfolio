## 🌟 Key Architecture & Highlights

- **Bespoke Design & Refined Typography**:
  - **Space Grotesk** for clean, futuristic tech headings.
  - **Plus Jakarta Sans** for crisp, highly legible body copy.
  - **JetBrains Mono** for numbers, terminal outputs, and metrics.
  - Deep obsidian (`#060911`) and electric sky / cyber-violet glow palette.
  - Interactive HTML5 constellation particle canvas (`js/particles.js`).
  - Genuine LinkedIn profile photo integrated with ambient glow frames.
- **Flagship Engineering Work & Case Studies**:
  - **Lumina — Clinical Decision Support Dashboard**: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Recharts, and Tailwind CSS. Simulates diagnostic imaging triage, evidence routing, and clinical telemetry. *Hackathon Flagship*.
  - **ELI5 This Page — Intelligent AI Chrome Extension**: Manifest V3 browser extension and Node.js/Express cloud microservice deployed on Render, powered by Google Gemini 3.6 Flash.
  - **Algorithmic Problem Solving & DSA Repository**: Daily problem-solving discipline in C++ cataloged with LeetHub-3.0 across LeetCode & Codolio.
- **LinkedIn-Grounded Academic Journey**:
  - **Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering (DJSCE)**: B.Tech in Information Technology (2025 – Present) with **9.9 CGPA** distinction.
  - **B. K. Birla College of Arts, Science & Commerce (Autonomous)**: Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) in Science (2023 – 2025).
  - **Secondary School Certificate (SSC)**: Completed March 2023.
- **Flawless Multi-Device Responsiveness**:
  - Optimized for mobile phones (320px+), tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide displays.
  - Touch-friendly controls with interactive mobile slide-down menu and outside-click dismissal.
- **Direct Reach & Clipboard Utility**:
  - 1-click email copy (`nityasunilmishra@gmail.com`) with animated floating toast.
  - Interactive pre-filled contact form linking directly to email compose.
  - Direct links to **LinkedIn**, **GitHub**, **LeetCode**, and **Codolio**.
